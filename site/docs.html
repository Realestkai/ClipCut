Install

Download the file for your computer.

  • Windows: run the setup.exe. ClipCut is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may stop it: press More info, then Run anyway.
  • Mac: open the .dmg and drag ClipCut into Applications. If macOS refuses to open it, run this once in Terminal:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ClipCut.app

Pick Apple silicon for an M1 or newer Mac, and Intel for an older one. Not sure? Apple menu › About This Mac says which chip you have.

Your first edit

  1. Open ClipCut and start a new project from the launch screen.
  2. Drag videos, photos and music into the media panel, then onto the timeline.
  3. Move the playhead to where a cut should be and split the clip. Drag a clip's edges to trim it.
  4. Stack clips on higher tracks for overlays and picture-in-picture.
  5. Add titles, effects, filters and transitions from the library and adjust them in the inspector.
  6. Press export when it looks right.
Projects save to your own disk. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Captions and voices

Captions, text-to-speech and background removal run on AI models that live on your computer. They are not bundled with the installer, to keep it small: download the ones you want once from Settings (the Transcriber and Speech pages), and they work offline from then on.

Bigger caption models are more accurate and slower. Start with a small one and move up if it mishears.

Export

ClipCut exports H.264, HEVC and AV1, up to 4K at 60 frames per second, with 10-bit colour where the format allows. For social media, H.264 at the resolution you shot in is the safe choice.

Shortcuts

Every key is listed, and can be changed, in Settings › Shortcuts.

Updates

Open Settings › Version and press Check for updates. ClipCut downloads the new version, checks it is intact and installs it over the one you are running. The same page lets you go back to an earlier version. What's new lists what each version changed.

Automation

ClipCut can be driven by scripts and AI agents. Turn it on in Settings › Automation: it listens on your own computer only (127.0.0.1) and answers only callers that hold its token. AI agents connect over MCP by starting the app with --mcp.

Privacy

There is no account and no telemetry. ClipCut reaches the network only when you ask it to: checking for updates, downloading a model, or turning Automation on.
