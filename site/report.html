Report a problem

Something broke, looks wrong or closed by itself? Send these four things and it can usually be fixed.

What you did and what happened

The steps that lead to the problem, what you expected, and what you got instead.

Your version

It is on Settings › About, and Settings › Version says whether a newer one is out. Try the newest first.

The log

Settings › About › Show the log opens the folder: attach the newest file. If ClipCut closed by itself, start it again first and attach the file before the newest.

Your computer

Settings › About › Copy system information, pasted into the report.

Open a report

Reports are public, so leave out anything private. A free account is needed to post one.
